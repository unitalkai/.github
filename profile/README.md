## Welcome to Unitalk

Unitalk enables companies, executives, and teams of all sizes to create, equip, and manage their **AI Collaborators**.

An AI Collaborator is not a disposable chatbot: it is a **durable company asset** with an embodied identity (chosen name, role, persistent memory, specialized business skills, corporate email/calendar, and strict permission boundaries).

### Public Open-Source Ecosystem

* 🖥️ **[unitalk-desktop](https://github.com/unitalkai/unitalk-desktop)**: The packaged desktop distribution of Hermes Agent, built for business teams. 1-click signed installers for Windows & macOS, unified LiteLLM AI Gateway with 10,000 free trial credits (€5), and Clerk Work SSO.
* 📦 **[profiles-distribution](https://github.com/unitalkai/profiles-distribution)**: Catalog of turnkey business personas, methods, and configurations.
* 🌐 **[unitalk.com](https://github.com/unitalkai/unitalk.com)**: The shared collaborative workspace bringing humans and AI Collaborators together.
* 🔌 **[hermes-plugins](https://github.com/unitalkai/hermes-plugins)**: Open plugins and business connectors.
* 🎯 **[unitalk-skills](https://github.com/unitalkai/unitalk-skills)**: Specialized business procedural memory modules.

---

### Key Features for Your Business

* **Single Embodied Persona**: Your Collaborator carries a single company-chosen name with native multimodal modules (**Images**, **Videos**, **Meetings**).
* **Unified AI Gateway**: Transparent routing across frontier models (Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5 Flash, GPT-4o) with dedicated credit quotas and BYOK support.
* **Corporate Sovereignty (AI in a Box)**: Deployable on private cloud or on-premise infrastructure with full managed services.
* **Continuous Memory & Knowledge**: Native integration with Honcho and vector knowledge bases (Qdrant).
